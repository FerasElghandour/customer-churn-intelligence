Explore the evidence

Illustrative customer inputs, not a real customer record. Margin 40%, discount 15%, contact cost £1.50.

How it works

Historical purchases → Defined churn label → Calibrated probability → Scenario economics

The saved benchmark evaluates 1,077 customers. Logistic regression ROC-AUC is 0.763. A bootstrap comparison does not establish a clear improvement from more complex candidates.

The £1.75m historical risk estimate is a model-based exposure measure. The £1.14m contact-list figure is modeled value at risk, not revenue saved. No retention treatment was observed.

The calculator holds the customer fixed while changing assumptions. It is not a new campaign measurement or a recommendation to contact a real person.

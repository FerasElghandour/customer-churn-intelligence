# Data and interpretation

This public demonstration uses retained aggregate findings; it publishes no new customer-level or client records. The source loader expects transaction data obtained separately from its original provider. Consult that provider's applicable terms before acquiring or reusing raw records.

Source: [UCI Online Retail II](https://archive.ics.uci.edu/dataset/502/online+retail+ii). The interactive calculator uses a synthetic customer and an assumed treatment response. Aggregates are historical analysis, not measured campaign outcomes.
